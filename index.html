Frequently Asked Questions

Where are Popular Jana Neethi Medicals & Surgicals stores located?

Popular Jana Neethi Medicals & Surgicals operates four outlets across Perinthalmanna and Angadippuram in Malappuram, Kerala: Jan Aushadhi Kendra on Calicut Road (Angadippuram 679321), Popular Jananeethi Medicals and Surgicals on Ootty Road SH-39 (Perinthalmanna 679322), Pradhan Mantri Jan Aushadhi Kendra in Shanti Nagar (Perinthalmanna 679322), and Prime Minister Jan Aushadhi Center opposite Moulana Hospitals (Perinthalmanna 679322).

Can I rent a hospital bed, wheelchair, or oxygen concentrator in Perinthalmanna?

Yes. Popular Jana Neethi Medicals & Surgicals rents sanitized Fowler and Semi-Fowler hospital beds, foldable and reclining wheelchairs, oxygen concentrators, BiPAP and CPAP machines, and air mattresses on affordable daily, weekly, or monthly rental plans across Perinthalmanna and Angadippuram.

Does Popular Jana Neethi offer home delivery for medicines?

Yes. You can order prescription medicines, Jan Aushadhi generics, and surgical supplies for safe doorstep home delivery by sending your prescription to Popular Jana Neethi Medicals & Surgicals via WhatsApp.

Is EMI available for buying medical equipment?

Yes. Popular Jana Neethi offers easy EMI shopping plans so families and clinics can buy essential high-value medical equipment—including oxygen concentrators, patient monitors, CPAP/BiPAP devices, and motorized hospital beds—in affordable monthly installments.

Do you sell Pradhan Mantri Jan Aushadhi generic medicines?

Yes. Our Jan Aushadhi Kendra branches in Angadippuram (Calicut Road), Shanti Nagar (Perinthalmanna), and opposite Moulana Hospitals stock quality-assured Pradhan Mantri Jan Aushadhi generic medicines alongside branded prescription drugs.

What are the opening hours of Popular Jana Neethi Medicals & Surgicals?

Popular Jana Neethi Medicals & Surgicals outlets open daily at 9:30 AM from Monday to Saturday.
